01 / The idea

Don't let useful water go to waste.

Water from washbasins and showers may be suitable for certain non-drinking uses after appropriate treatment and testing.

🚿

The Problem

Large volumes of lightly used water leave hostels and campuses every day.

⚙️

The Solution

Collect, treat, check and redirect suitable water for approved reuse.

🌱

The Outcome

Reduce demand for fresh water and support more responsible water use.

02 / The water journey

From drain to second life.

Five stages. One smarter water cycle.

🚿
STEP 01

Collection

Capture suitable basin and shower water separately.

🧺
STEP 02

Screening

Remove hair, lint and larger particles.

🪨
STEP 03

Filtration

Use suitable filtration and treatment stages.

🔬
STEP 04

Quality Check

Test water against standards for its intended use.

🌿
STEP 05

Reuse

Send approved water to suitable non-potable uses.

03 / Smart water system

Water in motion.
Waste in decline.

A collection network feeds a treatment unit. After quality checks, approved water moves to a dedicated storage tank and non-potable reuse lines.
